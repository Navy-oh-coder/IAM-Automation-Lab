# Entra ID Reporting

This area is intended to provide repeatable reporting for identity hygiene, group inventory, licensing and governance outcomes.

## Planned reporting areas

- Group inventory, membership and ownership.
- Licence assignment and utilisation.
- Stale user and guest review.
- Missing identity attributes.
- Lifecycle completion, exceptions and audit evidence.
- Governance metrics and outstanding remediation.

## Current status

Reporting capabilities are a mixture of existing scripts and planned documentation. Consult each subfolder's README for the specific status and known limitations. A generated CSV alone is not evidence that a report is complete or correct.

## Reporting standards

Reports should identify their collection time, scope, relevant limitations and errors. Distinguish a confirmed finding from unavailable data or a failed query. Do not store production exports or unnecessary personal data in the repository.
